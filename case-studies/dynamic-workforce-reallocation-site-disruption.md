# Case Study 01 — Dynamic Workforce Reallocation During Major Site Disruption

**Role:** Workforce Management / Command Center  
**Scope:** CSR & TSR Operations  
**Scale:** 300+ agents reskilled · 500+ queues supported  
**Outcome:** Abandon rate reduced from **11% → 4%** · **95% service-level target achieved by EOD**

> **Confidentiality note:** Client, site, and proprietary system details are intentionally omitted. This case study focuses on WFM decision-making and approved operational outcomes.

## Situation

An earthquake and subsequent fire caused one of the organization's largest operational sites to become completely unavailable. The affected site represented the organization's largest concentration of CSR and TSR headcount, creating an immediate staffing and customer-service risk across the remaining operation.

## WFM Challenge

- Significant loss of CSR and TSR capacity
- Increased pressure across 500+ queues
- Rising abandon-rate risk
- Service-level risk
- Need to redistribute available agents across LOBs
- Potential IVR and call-routing changes

## Response

### 1. Identify available capacity
Reviewed LOBs with high agent availability and coordinated with leaders to confirm which agents were cross-trained and eligible to support impacted queues. After approval, the required reskilling was processed.

**300+ agents were dynamically reskilled.**

### 2. Prioritize queues using real-time data
Reviewed all queues across the CSR and TSR LOBs. Capacity was prioritized using:

- **ASA (Average Speed of Answer)**
- **Calls waiting**
- **Abandon rate**

This directed available capacity toward queues experiencing the greatest immediate customer impact.

### 3. Activate AHOD — All Hands on Deck
Implemented **AHOD (All Hands on Deck)** to maximize production capacity. Where operationally appropriate, off-phone activities such as coaching, meetings, and training were cancelled or deferred.

### 4. Optimize breaks and lunches
Reviewed and optimized break and lunch schedules to improve intraday coverage while maintaining operational requirements.

### 5. Solicit additional OT
After internal capacity was optimized, solicited overtime support from other sites, subject to required leadership approvals.

### 6. Coordinate with IT
Coordinated with IT on necessary backend IVR messaging and routing changes where required.

### 7. Maintain real-time stakeholder communication
Provided real-time queue updates to stakeholders and leadership, covering queue conditions, staffing actions, risks, and intervention effectiveness.

## Decision Framework

```text
High-availability LOBs
        ↓
Validate cross-trained agents with leaders
        ↓
Approve + process dynamic reskilling
        ↓
Monitor all CSR / TSR queues
        ↓
Prioritize by ASA + Calls Waiting + Abandon Rate
        ↓
Apply capacity levers
  ├─ AHOD
  ├─ Break / lunch optimization
  ├─ Cross-site OT
  └─ IVR / routing coordination
        ↓
Real-time stakeholder updates
        ↓
Continuous reassessment
```

## Results

| Metric | Result |
|---|---:|
| **Abandon rate** | **11% → 4%** |
| **Service level** | **95% target achieved by EOD** |
| **Agents reskilled** | **300+** |
| **Queues supported** | **500+** |

The combined WFM interventions helped reduce abandon rate by **7 percentage points** and achieve the **95% service-level target by end of day**, despite the sudden loss of a major operating site.

## What This Demonstrates

- **Real-Time WFM:** intraday capacity management, dynamic reskilling, queue prioritization, AHOD, break/lunch optimization, and OT coordination.
- **Data-driven decision making:** ASA, calls waiting, and abandon rate used to prioritize intervention.
- **Crisis & business continuity:** maintaining customer-facing performance during a major site disruption.
- **Cross-functional leadership:** coordination across WFM, operations leadership, IT, site leaders, and stakeholders.

## Key Takeaway

> **We didn't simply react to the site outage. We treated available workforce capacity as a dynamic resource and continuously moved that capacity toward the queues experiencing the greatest customer impact.**
