Case Study 02 · Intraday Operations

Intraday Performance Management & Queue Stabilization

A practical WFM decision framework for identifying developing queue risk, assessing available capacity, coordinating interventions, and continuously reassessing performance throughout the day.

Real-Time OperationsQueue ManagementCapacity Optimization
Situation

Intraday conditions can change fast.

In a large contact center environment, call demand, staffing availability, adherence, and queue performance can shift throughout the day. The WFM challenge is not simply to report what happened, but to identify where risk is developing and translate the data into timely operational action.

Challenge

Signals that require attention

  • Multiple queues competing for available capacity
  • Changing staffing conditions
  • Service-level risk
  • Customer-impact risk
  • Need for rapid Operations alignment
Decision process

Turn real-time signals into action.

The framework below reflects the operating logic used to connect queue conditions with available capacity and appropriate interventions.

01

Monitor real-time queue conditions

Review queue conditions and intraday performance indicators. Key signals include ASA, calls waiting, abandon rate, service level, agent availability, adherence, and staffing conditions.

02

Prioritize the highest-impact queues

Rather than distributing capacity evenly, prioritize queues showing the strongest combination of immediate demand and customer impact.

03

Coordinate staffing actions

When capacity is available, coordinate appropriate actions with operational leaders. Depending on conditions, this can include eligible cross-trained capacity, activity adjustments, intraday schedule optimization, or additional support.

04

Maintain continuous communication

Provide real-time updates so Operations and leadership understand queue conditions, actions underway, risks, and whether interventions are improving performance.

05

Reassess and adjust

Treat WFM decisions as iterative. Review the operation again after an intervention and redirect capacity if the highest-priority risk changes.

Operating model

A simple loop for intraday control.

Real-Time Data→Identify Queue Risk→Assess Capacity→Validate Staffing→Intervene→Monitor Impact→Reallocate / Escalate
Business value

WFM as an active decision function.

This approach connects operational data to action instead of treating WFM as reporting-only. Continuous visibility into queue pressure and available capacity supports faster responses to changing intraday risk and keeps customer-impact measures visible to leadership.
