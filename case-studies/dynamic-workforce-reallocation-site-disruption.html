Case Study 01 · Crisis Response

Dynamic Workforce Reallocation During Major Site Disruption

Using real-time capacity, dynamic reskilling, AHOD, schedule optimization, OT, IT coordination, and stakeholder communication to stabilize customer-facing operations after a major site became unavailable.

Situation

A sudden loss of capacity

An earthquake and subsequent fire caused one of the organization's largest operational sites to become completely unavailable. The affected site represented the organization's largest concentration of CSR and TSR headcount, creating an immediate staffing and customer-service risk across the remaining operation.

Challenge

What had to be protected

  • CSR and TSR capacity
  • 500+ queues
  • Service-level performance
  • Abandon rate
  • Customer access to support
  • Potential IVR and routing requirements
Response

Capacity was treated as a dynamic resource.

The response focused on finding eligible capacity, directing it toward the highest customer-impact queues, and continuously reassessing the operation.

01

Identify available capacity

Reviewed LOBs with high agent availability and coordinated with leaders to confirm which agents were cross-trained and eligible to support impacted queues. After approval, dynamic reskilling was processed. 300+ agents were reskilled.

02

Prioritize queues using real-time data

Reviewed all queues across the CSR and TSR LOBs. Capacity was prioritized using ASA, calls waiting, and abandon rate to direct available staffing toward the greatest immediate customer impact.

03

Activate AHOD

Implemented AHOD (All Hands on Deck) to maximize production capacity. Where operationally appropriate, coaching, meetings, and training were cancelled or deferred.

04

Optimize breaks and lunches

Reviewed and optimized break and lunch schedules to improve intraday coverage while maintaining operational requirements.

05

Solicit additional OT

After internal capacity was optimized, solicited overtime support from other sites, subject to required leadership approvals.

06

Coordinate with IT

Coordinated with IT on necessary backend IVR messaging and routing changes where required.

07

Maintain real-time stakeholder communication

Provided real-time queue updates to stakeholders and leadership, covering queue conditions, staffing actions, risks, and intervention effectiveness.

Decision framework

From queue pressure to intervention.

High-availability LOBs→Validate cross-trained agents→Approve + reskill→Monitor CSR / TSR queues→ASA + Calls Waiting + Abandon→AHOD / Breaks / OT / Routing→Reassess
