Featured automation project

Skilling Request Automation

A practical Power Automate workflow inspired by real workforce management operations.

Problem

Manual processing of workforce requests required repetitive data entry and follow-up, slowing down response time and increasing the risk of errors.

Solution

Built a Power Automate workflow that captures form submissions directly into an Excel tracker, supports both single and bulk entries, and automatically triggers Teams and email notifications to keep stakeholders informed.

Result

Reduced manual data entry and follow-up effort, giving the team faster visibility and more consistent handling of requests.
