# 📊 Featured Case Study

## Dynamic Workforce Reallocation During Major Site Disruption

**Role:** Workforce Management / Command Center  
**Scope:** CSR & TSR Operations

### Situation

An earthquake and subsequent fire caused one of the organization's largest operational sites to become completely unavailable.

The affected site represented the organization's largest concentration of **CSR and TSR headcount**, creating an immediate staffing and customer-service risk across the remaining operation.

### Operational Challenge

The disruption created:

- Significant loss of CSR and TSR capacity
- Increased pressure across **500+ queues**
- Rising abandon-rate risk
- Service-level risk
- Need to redistribute available agents across LOBs
- Potential IVR and call-routing requirements

### Response

#### 1. Identify Available Capacity

Reviewed LOBs with high agent availability and coordinated with leaders to determine which agents were cross-trained and eligible to support impacted queues.

Once approval was received, agents were dynamically reskilled.

**Result: 300+ agents reskilled.**

#### 2. Prioritize Queues Using Real-Time Data

Reviewed queues across the CSR and TSR LOBs.

Capacity was prioritized based on:

- **ASA (Average Speed of Answer)**
- **Number of calls waiting**
- **Abandon rate**

This allowed available capacity to be directed toward queues experiencing the greatest immediate customer impact.

#### 3. Activate AHOD

Implemented **AHOD (All Hands on Deck)** to maximize available production capacity.

Where operationally appropriate, off-phone activities such as:

- Coaching
- Meetings
- Training

were cancelled or deferred.

#### 4. Optimize Breaks & Lunches

Reviewed and optimized break and lunch schedules to improve intraday coverage while maintaining operational requirements.

#### 5. Solicit Additional OT

After internal capacity was optimized, solicited overtime support from other sites, subject to required leadership approval.

#### 6. Coordinate With IT

Coordinated with IT regarding necessary backend **IVR messaging and call-routing changes**, where required.

#### 7. Maintain Real-Time Stakeholder Communication

Provided real-time queue and operational updates to stakeholders and leadership, including:

- Queue conditions
- Staffing actions
- Emerging risks
- Intervention effectiveness

---

## 📈 Results

| Metric | Result |
|---|---:|
| Agents dynamically reskilled | **300+** |
| Queues supported | **500+** |
| Abandon rate | **11% → 4%** |
| Service level | **95% target achieved by EOD** |

### Business Impact

The response helped stabilize customer-facing operations following a major site disruption by rapidly reallocating available workforce capacity toward the queues experiencing the greatest immediate demand.

The combination of **dynamic reskilling, AHOD activation, schedule optimization, additional OT, IT coordination, and real-time stakeholder communication** enabled the operation to achieve the **95% service-level target by EOD** while reducing abandon rate from **11% to 4%**.
